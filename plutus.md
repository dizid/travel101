- **Unauthorized charges** - Multiple reports of being charged after cancellation or downgrading
- **Poor customer support** - Unresponsive or unhelpful support team
- **Misleading cashback promises** - Advertised cashback rates not matching actual returns
- **Subscription traps** - Difficulty canceling or unexpected charges for subscriptions
